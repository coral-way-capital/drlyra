⚡ Proposal — Alternatives to Purple Not adopted — for review

Purple/violet paired with dark mode has become the default "AI insight" gradient across the industry — it no longer differentiates Dr. Lyra. Green stays (it's the Spotify link, earned not arbitrary); these three directions swap the purple for something more specific to a psychologist decoding your music. Each is shown as it would render in the archetype heading.

Direction A
Amber Clay
The Luminous Nomad

Warm, human, editorial — reads like a therapist's handwriting, not a tech dashboard. Ties to vinyl warmth and analog nostalgia.

01 · Core Personality Archetype
The Luminous Nomad
"A restless seeker who uses sound as both compass and shelter."

You don't listen to music passively — you migrate through it. Your library reveals someone who processes the world through aesthetic intensity, drawn equally to the precision of electronic minimalism and the raw emotional weight of confessional songwriting.

This isn't contradiction. It's the hallmark of a mind that refuses to be reduced to a single frequency.
